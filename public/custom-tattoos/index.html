Questions people ask

Can I get a custom tattoo?

Yes. Bring your idea, reference photos and where you want it, and your artist will draw a design that is yours.

Can I bring my own design?

Yes. Bring your drawing or reference photos and talk it through with your artist. They will help make it work as a tattoo.

How do I pick the right artist?

Look through each artist's Instagram, which is where they post the most work, and pick the one whose style matches what you want. Kira does neotraditional and illustrative. Tanis does traditional and neo traditional. Kayla does bright, colorful and pop culture work. Jimmy does American and Japanese traditional and lettering. Ivy does black and grey and fine line. Christian does bold traditional and fine line.

What tattoo styles do you do?

American traditional, neo traditional, neotraditional, illustrative, Japanese traditional, lettering, black and grey, fine line, geometric, dotwork, ornamental, color work, anime and pop culture. Each artist's page shows their work.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Every artist prices their own work, so the price depends on who you pick and what you want. Tattoos start at $150. Send your artist your idea, the size and where it goes on your body, and they will give you a quote.

Do I need a deposit?

Some of our artists take a deposit to hold your appointment. Deposits are non-refundable.

How long does a tattoo take?

It depends on the size and detail. A small piece can take under an hour. Big pieces can take several sessions. Your artist will give you an idea when you book.

How do I book?

Each artist and our piercer book on their own. Pick the artist whose work you like and use the Contact Me button on their page. Some book through a form and some through an Instagram message. You can also walk in during open hours.

How should I get ready for my tattoo?

Get a good night of sleep, eat a real meal before you come in, drink water and wear clothes that make the spot easy to get to. Your artist will go over aftercare when you are done.
