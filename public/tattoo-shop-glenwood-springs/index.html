Tattoo shop in Glenwood Springs, Colorado

Holy Iron Tattoo is on Grand Ave in downtown Glenwood Springs. Six tattoo artists and a piercer, open every day from 11am to 7pm. Walk-ins are 12 to 7 every day, and we take appointments too.

It is the shop for the people who live here, from Glenwood to Rifle to Aspen. Come in, shoot some pool, look at the local art and books, and talk through your idea with an artist.

Questions people ask

Where is Holy Iron Tattoo?

We are at 824 Grand Ave in downtown Glenwood Springs, Colorado 81601. We are open every day, 11am to 7pm. Call us at (970) 230-9366.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are 12pm to 7pm every day, and we take appointments too. Event days and holidays can be different, so check our Instagram, @holyirontattoo. If you want a big custom piece, book with your artist ahead of time so they can draw it for you.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Every artist prices their own work, so the price depends on who you pick and what you want. Tattoos start at $150. Send your artist your idea, the size and where it goes on your body, and they will give you a quote.

Do you have a shop minimum?

Yes. Tattoos start at $150. Your artist will give you the exact price for your piece.

How old do I have to be to get a tattoo?

You have to be 18 or older to get tattooed at Holy Iron.

Do I need a deposit?

Some of our artists take a deposit to hold your appointment. Deposits are non-refundable.

How do I book?

Each artist and our piercer book on their own. Pick the artist whose work you like and use the Contact Me button on their page. Some book through a form and some through an Instagram message. You can also walk in during open hours.

Can I bring friends to my appointment?

Please bring just one friend if you can.

How do I pick the right artist?

Look through each artist's Instagram, which is where they post the most work, and pick the one whose style matches what you want. Kira does neotraditional and illustrative. Tanis does traditional and neo traditional. Kayla does bright, colorful and pop culture work. Jimmy does American and Japanese traditional and lettering. Ivy does black and grey and fine line. Christian does bold traditional and fine line.

What tattoo styles do you do?

American traditional, neo traditional, neotraditional, illustrative, Japanese traditional, lettering, black and grey, fine line, geometric, dotwork, ornamental, color work, anime and pop culture. Each artist's page shows their work.

What is the shop like?

Holy Iron is a welcoming, positive shop on Grand Ave. There is a pool table, movies playing, a place to hang out, and local art and books for sale. It is the shop for people who live and work in the valley.

Were you White Lodge Tattoo?

Yes. Holy Iron Tattoo used to be White Lodge Tattoo and Gallery. Same spot on Grand Ave in Glenwood Springs.

Who owns Holy Iron Tattoo?

Kira Knowlton owns Holy Iron, so we are a women-owned shop. She is also one of our tattoo artists.

When should I book ahead?

Winter around the holidays and all of summer are our busiest times. Book ahead for those. Fall and spring are quieter, which is a great time to get in fast.

How far is Holy Iron from Rifle?

About 30 minutes east of Rifle on I-70.
