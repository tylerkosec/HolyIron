Questions people ask

Where is Holy Iron Tattoo?

We are at 824 Grand Ave in downtown Glenwood Springs, Colorado 81601. We are open every day, 11am to 7pm. Call us at (970) 230-9366.

What are your hours?

We are open every day of the week, 11am to 7pm.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are 12pm to 7pm every day, and we take appointments too. Event days and holidays can be different, so check our Instagram, @holyirontattoo. If you want a big custom piece, book with your artist ahead of time so they can draw it for you.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Every artist prices their own work, so the price depends on who you pick and what you want. Tattoos start at $150. Send your artist your idea, the size and where it goes on your body, and they will give you a quote.

Do you have a shop minimum?

Yes. Tattoos start at $150. Your artist will give you the exact price for your piece.

How old do I have to be to get a tattoo?

You have to be 18 or older to get tattooed at Holy Iron.

Can I bring friends to my appointment?

Please bring just one friend if you can.

Do I need a deposit?

Some of our artists take a deposit to hold your appointment. Deposits are non-refundable.

How do I book?

Each artist and our piercer book on their own. Pick the artist whose work you like and use the Contact Me button on their page. Some book through a form and some through an Instagram message. You can also walk in during open hours.

How do I pick the right artist?

Look through each artist's Instagram, which is where they post the most work, and pick the one whose style matches what you want. Kira does neotraditional and illustrative. Tanis does traditional and neo traditional. Kayla does bright, colorful and pop culture work. Jimmy does American and Japanese traditional and lettering. Ivy does black and grey and fine line. Christian does bold traditional and fine line.

What tattoo styles do you do?

American traditional, neo traditional, neotraditional, illustrative, Japanese traditional, lettering, black and grey, fine line, geometric, dotwork, ornamental, color work, anime and pop culture. Each artist's page shows their work.

Can I get a custom tattoo?

Yes. Bring your idea, reference photos and where you want it, and your artist will draw a design that is yours.

Can I bring my own design?

Yes. Bring your drawing or reference photos and talk it through with your artist. They will help make it work as a tattoo.

Do you do cover-ups?

Yes. Bring a clear photo of the old tattoo, or come in, and an artist will tell you what can go over it. Darker and bolder designs usually cover best.

Can you fix or rework an old tattoo?

Yes. Old tattoos can often be touched up, reworked or brought back to life. Come in or send your artist a photo and they will tell you what is possible.

Do you do touch-ups?

Yes. Touch-ups are up to each artist, so reach out to the artist who did your tattoo, or come in and we will take a look.

What is a flash day?

A flash day is when our artists put out sheets of ready-made designs at set prices and tattoo them first come, first served. We do flash events on most holidays and every Friday the 13th. We post them on Instagram at @holyirontattoo.

When is your next flash day?

Watch our Instagram, @holyirontattoo. That is where every flash day gets posted first.

Do you do piercings?

Yes. Mateo Gil de Rubio is our piercer. He pierces Friday through Monday. Message him on Instagram at @untotheblade to book.

What piercings do you do?

Mateo does ear piercings like the helix, forward helix, conch, rook, flat, tragus, surface tragus and transverse lobe, plus nostril, septum, eyebrow, lip and navel piercings.

How much does a piercing cost?

It depends on the piercing and the jewelry you pick. Message Mateo on Instagram at @untotheblade with what you want and he will give you a price.

How old do I have to be to get pierced?

Mateo pierces anyone 18 and up. If you are 16 or 17, he does earlobes only, with consent from a parent or guardian. He does not pierce anyone under 16.

What jewelry do you use for piercings?

Mateo uses titanium and solid 14k gold jewelry, including pieces from Junipurr Jewelry and Tilum.

What is the shop like?

Holy Iron is a welcoming, positive shop on Grand Ave. There is a pool table, movies playing, a place to hang out, and local art and books for sale. It is the shop for people who live and work in the valley.

Do you sell art?

Yes. We carry art and books by local artists in the shop.

Were you White Lodge Tattoo?

Yes. Holy Iron Tattoo used to be White Lodge Tattoo and Gallery. Same spot on Grand Ave in Glenwood Springs.

Who owns Holy Iron Tattoo?

Kira Knowlton owns Holy Iron, so we are a women-owned shop. She is also one of our tattoo artists.

When should I book ahead?

Winter around the holidays and all of summer are our busiest times. Book ahead for those. Fall and spring are quieter, which is a great time to get in fast.

How long does a tattoo take?

It depends on the size and detail. A small piece can take under an hour. Big pieces can take several sessions. Your artist will give you an idea when you book.

How should I get ready for my tattoo?

Get a good night of sleep, eat a real meal before you come in, drink water and wear clothes that make the spot easy to get to. Your artist will go over aftercare when you are done.

How far is Holy Iron from Carbondale and Basalt?

About 25 minutes from Carbondale and about 35 minutes from Basalt, down Highway 82 into downtown Glenwood Springs.

How far is Holy Iron from Aspen?

About an hour down Highway 82. A lot of people who work in Aspen and Vail live in Glenwood, and this is their shop.

Is there a good tattoo shop near Aspen?

Holy Iron Tattoo in Glenwood Springs is about an hour from Aspen. Six tattoo artists in every style from traditional to fine line, a piercer, and open every day.

How far is Holy Iron from Rifle?

About 30 minutes east of Rifle on I-70.

How far is Holy Iron from Vail and Eagle?

About 45 minutes from Eagle and about an hour from Vail, west on I-70 through Glenwood Canyon.

Is there a tattoo shop near Vail or Eagle?

Holy Iron Tattoo in downtown Glenwood Springs is under an hour from Eagle and about an hour from Vail. Six tattoo artists, a piercer, walk-ins and appointments, open every day.

How far is Holy Iron from Grand Junction?

About an hour and a half east on I-70. You come out right by downtown Glenwood Springs, a few minutes from the shop on Grand Ave.

Is it worth driving from Grand Junction for a tattoo?

You get to pick an artist by their work, talk through a custom piece and spend the day in Glenwood with the hot springs, food and the river right there.

How do I plan a trip from Grand Junction for a tattoo?

Pick your artist, book with them ahead of time and send your idea, size and placement. Then you can drive over knowing your spot is held. Walk-ins are welcome too if you want to take a chance.
